OurServices

Course Companion provides educators with a structured platform to create and manage courses, upload learning materials, and support students through content-based AI assistance.

The system enables efficient enrollment management while ensuring learning remains accurate, secure, and instructor-driven.

+917356913830

Call us direct 24/7 for get a free consultation

Course creation

The course creation feature enables educators to design well-structured courses with clearly defined content and learning flow.

Material upload

Material upload enables educators to securely add and organize course resources such as documents, videos.

AI assistance

AI-based doubt support helps learners clarify questions using responses generated strictly from the educator’s uploaded course materials.

Enrollment tracking

Enrollment management allows educators to review, approve, and monitor student participation across their courses.
